Why Choose a
Family Holidays?

India is a land of wonder for children and adults alike, and Aaradhya Tours specialises in creating family holidays that engage, educate, and thrill every generation. Whether it's spotting a Bengal tiger in Ranthambore, riding an elephant in Jaipur, building sandcastles in Goa, or releasing turtles on the Andaman coast, India offers family experiences that are unlike anything else in the world.

We understand family travel — the need for child-friendly hotels with pools, flexible meal timings, age-appropriate activities, and a pace that doesn't exhaust the youngest members of the group. Our family packages come with experienced guides who are wonderful with children, and every itinerary is designed to create stories that families will retell for decades.
