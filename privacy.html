Effective Date: 1 January 2026  ·  Aaradhya Tours Pvt. Ltd.

Aaradhya Tours Pvt. Ltd. is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information. By using our website or booking a tour, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when booking tours, making enquiries, or contacting our team. This includes:

  • Full name, email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Travel dates, destination preferences, and special requirements
  • Payment information (processed securely via third-party gateways — we do not store card numbers)
  • Passport and identification details where required for visa applications or permits
  • Communications with our team via email, WhatsApp, or website forms

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely to:

  • Process your tour bookings and related travel arrangements
  • Communicate regarding your trips, including confirmations, updates, and emergency contacts
  • Comply with legal obligations including tourism regulations and financial reporting
  • Send relevant travel offers and newsletters (only if you have opted in)
  • Improve our services through anonymised analytics

3. Data Sharing

Aaradhya Tours does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share your data only with:

  • Hotel and accommodation partners (name, arrival date only)
  • Transport operators (name, contact number as required)
  • Government authorities where legally required (immigration, FRRO)
  • Payment processors (PCI-DSS compliant partners only)

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted staff access to protect your personal information. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure and we encourage users to contact us directly for highly sensitive information.

5. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality (session management, preferences) and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain site features.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Booking records are retained for 7 years per Indian financial regulations. You may request deletion of your data after this period.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related queries, data requests, or concerns, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@aaradhyatours.com or write to: Aaradhya Tours Pvt. Ltd., 12 Heritage Plaza, MG Road, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302001.
