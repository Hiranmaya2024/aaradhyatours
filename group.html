Why Choose a
Group Tours?

There is something uniquely magical about exploring India with a group of fellow travellers. Shared wonder, unexpected friendships, communal laughter at a camel's stubborn refusal to move — group travel creates a different kind of memory. Aaradhya Tours runs fixed-departure group tours every month to India's top destinations, with group sizes of 12–20 people for the best balance of community and flexibility.

Our group tours offer exceptional value without compromising on quality — shared costs mean premium hotels and experiences at prices that would be impossible for individuals. Each tour is led by one of our senior, personally vetted Tour Managers who is with you from airport to airport, ensuring everything runs smoothly while leaving you free to simply enjoy.
