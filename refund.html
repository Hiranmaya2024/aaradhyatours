Effective Date: 1 January 2026  ·  Aaradhya Tours Pvt. Ltd.

Aaradhya Tours understands that travel plans sometimes change, and we aim to handle cancellations with fairness and transparency. Please review our cancellation policy carefully before booking. We recommend purchasing travel insurance to protect your investment against unforeseen circumstances.

1. Cancellation by the Traveller

If you need to cancel your booking, cancellation charges apply based on the number of days before your travel start date:

  • 61 days or more: Full refund minus 10% administrative fee
  • 46–60 days: 75% refund of total package cost
  • 31–45 days: 50% refund of total package cost
  • 16–30 days: 25% refund of total package cost
  • 0–15 days: No refund (100% cancellation charge)
  • No-show: No refund under any circumstances

2. How to Cancel

  • Send a written cancellation request to bookings@aaradhyatours.com
  • Include your booking reference number and full name
  • Cancellations are effective from the date the written request is received
  • Verbal cancellations over phone are not accepted as formal cancellations

3. Special Non-Refundable Components

Certain components of tour packages are non-refundable regardless of cancellation notice:

  • Char Dham Yatra helicopter bookings (subject to helicopter operator policy)
  • Leh-Ladakh inner line permits and zone bookings
  • Festival season hotel deposits (Pushkar Fair, Diwali in Varanasi, etc.)
  • International airfare components where booked as part of a package
  • Train tickets booked in Tatkal quota or special express trains
  • Advance boat or houseboat bookings during peak Kerala season

4. Cancellation by Aaradhya Tours

In the rare event that we must cancel a tour due to insufficient group size (for group tours), force majeure, or safety concerns, we will:

  • Notify you at the earliest possible opportunity
  • Offer an alternative tour of comparable value
  • Provide a full credit note valid for 12 months from the cancellation date
  • Offer a full refund if the alternative tour is not acceptable to you
  • Note: In cases of force majeure, refunds are subject to recovery from hotel and transport partners and may be partial

5. Rescheduling Policy

  • One free rescheduling is permitted if requested 45+ days before travel start date
  • Rescheduling within 44–30 days: ₹2,500 administrative fee per person
  • Rescheduling within 29–15 days: ₹5,000 administrative fee per person
  • Rescheduling within 14 days: Not permitted — only cancellation applies
  • Rescheduled travel must be completed within 12 months of original travel date

6. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 working days
  • Refunds are made to the original payment method (card/bank account)
  • Credit card refunds may take an additional 5–7 business days to reflect
  • We will send you a refund confirmation email with transaction reference

7. Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance at the time of booking. Travel insurance can cover costs arising from medical emergencies, trip interruption, and cancellation due to personal emergencies — situations that may not qualify for a refund under this policy. We offer travel insurance through our authorised insurance partner at the time of booking.

8. Contact for Refund Queries

For all cancellation and refund queries, please contact: refunds@aaradhyatours.com or call +91 98765 43210 (Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–7 PM IST).
