✦ About Andaman Islands

Andaman Islands — An Overview

The Andaman Islands are India's best-kept secret — an archipelago of 572 islands in the Bay of Bengal, most of them uninhabited and protected. With some of the world's clearest waters, untouched coral reefs, and beaches that rival the Maldives, the Andamans offer a tropical paradise experience that is still relatively off the beaten path.

Havelock Island (officially Swaraj Dweep) hosts Radhanagar Beach, repeatedly voted Asia's best beach. Neil Island is quieter and more rustic, while Port Blair — the state capital — offers historical depth through the haunting Cellular Jail, a reminder of India's freedom struggle. World-class snorkelling, scuba diving, sea-walking, and bioluminescent beach experiences make the Andamans a bucket-list destination.

✦ Highlights

Top Experiences in Andaman Islands

🤿
Scuba Diving
Crystal-clear waters with 50+ dive sites — explore vibrant coral gardens, nurse sharks, manta rays, and sea turtles at Havelock and Barracuda City.
🏖️
Radhanagar Beach
Asia's best beach — a 2 km arc of white sand fringed by tropical forest, with a flawless sunset that has made it legendary.
🌟
Bioluminescent Beach
At night, the shores of Havelock glow with blue bioluminescent plankton — one of nature's most magical phenomena.
🏛️
Cellular Jail Light Show
The haunting Cellular Jail in Port Blair, where Indian freedom fighters were imprisoned — attend the nightly Sound & Light Show.
🚣
Glass-Bottom Boat
Glide over coral reefs in a glass-bottom boat tour around Jolly Buoy Island, one of India's finest marine national parks.
🐢
Sea Walking
Walk on the ocean floor 5 metres below sea level without scuba training — a unique, accessible marine experience.
