✦ About Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh — An Overview

Himachal Pradesh, nestled in the western Himalayas, is a state of extraordinary contrasts. From the colonial charm of Shimla — once the summer capital of British India — to the snow-dusted Rohtang Pass above Manali, and from the Tibetan monastery town of McLeod Ganj to the serene Spiti Valley, Himachal is a paradise for every kind of traveller.

The state's apple orchards, cedar forests, rushing rivers, and ancient deodar temples create landscapes of surpassing beauty. Adventure seekers come for paragliding at Bir Billing, white-water rafting on the Beas, and trekking in the Pir Panjal range, while those seeking peace find it in the meditation centres of Dharamshala and the remote villages of Kinnaur.

✦ Highlights

Top Experiences in Himachal Pradesh

🏔️
Rohtang Pass
Cross the legendary Rohtang Pass (3,978m) above Manali for jaw-dropping Himalayan vistas and the first snowfall experience.
🧘
McLeod Ganj
The home of the Dalai Lama — explore Tibetan temples, monasteries, cafes, and the Institute of Buddhist Dialectics.
🪂
Paragliding at Bir Billing
Soar over the Himalayan foothills from Bir Billing, the world's second-highest paragliding site.
🌸
Shimla Heritage Walk
Stroll the famous Mall Road, explore the Viceregal Lodge, Jakhoo Temple, and the charming colonial architecture.
🍎
Spiti Valley
The remote, high-altitude cold desert valley of Spiti with its whitewashed monasteries and dramatic lunar landscapes.
🚂
Toy Train
Ride the UNESCO-listed Kalka-Shimla Narrow Gauge Railway through 103 tunnels and 864 bridges.
