✦ About Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand — An Overview

Uttarakhand, revered as Devbhumi (Land of the Gods), is where the Ganges and Yamuna rivers descend from the Himalayas, and where four of Hinduism's most sacred shrines — the Char Dham — draw millions of pilgrims each year. The state's towering peaks, including Nanda Devi, India's second-highest mountain, form a breathtaking backdrop to this profoundly spiritual land.

Beyond its religious significance, Uttarakhand is home to India's first national park (Corbett), the global yoga capital of Rishikesh, the hill stations of Mussoorie and Nainital, and some of the Himalaya's finest trekking routes including the Valley of Flowers, Roopkund, and Kedarkantha. It is a state where adventure and spirituality coexist in perfect harmony.

✦ Highlights

Top Experiences in Uttarakhand

🙏
Char Dham Yatra
Complete the sacred Char Dham circuit — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath — the holiest pilgrimage in Hinduism.
🧘
Rishikesh
The yoga capital of the world — learn authentic yoga and meditation, attend Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat, and explore Beatle ashram.
🐯
Jim Corbett
India's oldest national park offers exceptional tiger sightings, elephant safaris, and rich birdlife in the Ramganga river valley.
🌸
Valley of Flowers
Trek to the UNESCO World Heritage Site Valley of Flowers — a high-altitude meadow carpeted in hundreds of wild flower species.
🏔️
Nainital & Mussoorie
Charming colonial hill stations with lakeside promenades, toy train rides, and spectacular Himalayan panoramas.
🚣
River Rafting
White-water raft the thrilling rapids of the Alaknanda and Ganges rivers between Shivpuri and Rishikesh.
