Why Choose a
Wildlife Safaris?

India is one of the world's great wildlife destinations, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger, Asiatic Lion, Indian One-Horned Rhinoceros, Snow Leopard, and over 1,300 species of birds. With 50+ national parks and countless wildlife sanctuaries spread across the subcontinent's diverse ecosystems — from Himalayan alpine forests to tropical rainforests and mangrove swamps — India offers safari experiences as varied as the landscapes themselves.

Aaradhya Tours works exclusively with government-licensed naturalists and certified wildlife guides. Our safari vehicles are unobtrusive and respectful of the animals and environment. We believe in low-impact, high-awareness wildlife tourism that supports conservation and the local communities who are the true guardians of India's wild places.
