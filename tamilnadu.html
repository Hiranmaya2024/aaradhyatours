✦ About Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu — An Overview

Tamil Nadu, in India's deep south, is the heartland of one of the world's oldest living civilisations. Its great temples — many over 2,000 years old — are masterpieces of Dravidian architecture, rising in towering gopurams (gateway towers) encrusted with thousands of sculpted deities. The state's classical music, Bharatanatyam dance, and Carnatic tradition represent the pinnacle of Indian artistic heritage.

From the magnificent Meenakshi Amman Temple at Madurai to the Brihadeeswarar Temple at Thanjavur (a UNESCO site), from the southernmost tip of India at Kanyakumari where three seas meet, to the colonial elegance of Chennai and the cool Nilgiris hills with their tea gardens and Toda tribal heritage, Tamil Nadu is a state of extraordinary depth and diversity.

✦ Highlights

Top Experiences in Tamil Nadu

🛕
Meenakshi Temple
Marvel at the 14-towered Meenakshi Amman Temple at Madurai — one of India's greatest architectural and religious wonders.
🏛️
Brihadeeswarar Temple
UNESCO-listed temple at Thanjavur, built 1,000 years ago — a triumph of Chola dynasty architecture that still astounds engineers.
🌅
Kanyakumari Sunrise
Stand at the southernmost tip of India where the Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean, and Bay of Bengal converge — watch sunrise and sunset.
🌿
Nilgiris & Ooty
Ride the UNESCO Nilgiri Mountain Railway to Ooty, explore tea estates, and encounter the unique Toda tribal culture.
🎭
Bharatanatyam
Attend a classical Bharatanatyam performance — the ancient temple dance tradition that originated in Tamil Nadu.
🧵
Silk & Handicrafts
Kanchipuram silk sarees are among the world's finest — visit the weavers directly in this ancient silk city near Chennai.
